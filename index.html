Sabihin

Tagalog for "say it."

Press a hotkey, speak, and polished text lands at your cursor in whatever app has focus. Punctuation, casing, numbers, and names come out right. Speech recognition, formatting, and screen-context handling all run locally.

It works the same on a plane, in a SCIF, or with the network unplugged. Nothing you say leaves the machine.

The Forge

Keeps your build honest.

An AI-native project manager that sits above your coding agent, Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex. It locks a spec, watches the build against it, and flags the moment an agent drifts from what you actually asked for.

Bring your own API key — Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or a local Ollama model. Keys are stored in the macOS Keychain and never leave your machine. No Forge account, no Forge server in the loop.

How we build

Three rules. Every product we ship follows all of them, or it doesn't ship.

It never touches our servers.

Sabihin runs the model on your hardware. The Forge and AR Mechanic run on whatever key you give them. None of them has a path back to us. There's nothing on our servers to breach, subpoena, or sell, because there's nothing on our servers.

It says what it does.

Plain language about what leaves your machine and what stays. Free tiers are actually free, not a funnel. Paid tiers are opt-in, not a trap. No dark patterns, no fine print doing the real talking.

It shows its work.

Sabihin gives you the same output for the same input, every time. The Forge locks a spec and keeps an audit trail of every deviation from it. You can always see what the software decided and why.

About

A small company making serious tools.

Orbital AI is a one-person software company based in the United States. We build for people who do serious work and don't want a vendor sitting between them and it, whether that's a dictated word or a week of code.

The name is the idea. The software orbits you: it runs on your terms, answers to you, and leaves when you say so. Two products now, a third in development. Whatever comes next follows the same three rules.
