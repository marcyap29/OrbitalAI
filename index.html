How we build

Three rules. Every product we ship follows all of them, or it doesn't ship.

It runs on your device.

The model, the processing, the data. All of it lives on hardware you own. If a feature needs a server, we don't build the feature.

You buy it once.

No subscription to keep the lights on. Pay for the software, get updates for a year, keep using it for as long as you like.

It says what it does.

Plain language about what leaves the device (nothing) and what stays (everything). No dark patterns, no fine print doing the real talking.

About

A small company making serious tools.

Orbital AI is a one-person software company based in the United States. We build for people who dictate, write, and think all day and who care where that work ends up. Most of them can't, or won't, hand their words to a cloud.

The name is the idea. The software orbits you: it runs where you are, it follows your rules, and it leaves when you say so. More products are on the way. Each one will follow the same three rules.
