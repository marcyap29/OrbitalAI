The Methodology

Two Frameworks.
One Validated Instrument.

RDF — Paper 1

Radial Disruption Field

Maps which industries and companies fall within a disruption's blast radius, using a substrate overlap decay model. The output is a scored blast radius with specific exposure levels per industry segment.

PAT — Paper 2

Pivot Adjacency Theory

Scores whether a post-disruption response will succeed or accelerate decline. Every pivot gets a number. Survival threshold is 0.45. A scored pivot of 0.043 and a scored pivot of 0.59 are not equivalent bets.

Both validated against 59 companies across 7 disruption categories, confirmed with Firth's penalized logistic regression and LOO cross-validation. | Published on SSRN
